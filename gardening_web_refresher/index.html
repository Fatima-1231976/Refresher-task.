Beginner gardening tips

Small steps can make it easier to care for a garden.

  • Check what amount of sunlight each plant needs.
  • Water plants when the soil needs it, rather than on a strict schedule.
  • Use pots with drainage holes to help prevent waterlogging.
  • Be patient: plants take time to grow.
